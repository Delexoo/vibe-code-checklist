Privacy policy

This policy describes the Vibe Code Checklist website. It does not describe an app you audit with the checklist.

What stays on your device

When you tick items or type a project name, the site saves that progress in local storage in your browser. The keys are vibe-code-checklist-v1 and vibe-code-checklist-project. Those values are not sent to a server operated by this project.

Copying the audit prompt uses your device clipboard. Downloading a report builds the file in your browser.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. There is no cookie banner because there is no non-essential cookie to accept or refuse.

Pages you leave

Source and contact links go to GitHub. GitHub's own policy applies there. Your editor, if you paste the prompt into one, is also outside this site.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the repository issues.

Vibe Code Checklist is a shipping guide. It is not a legal opinion.
